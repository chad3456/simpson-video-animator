# Look and sound

The contract for every episode of *Dostoevsky at the Coin Laundry*. An original homage to quiet hand-painted slice-of-life anime; no frames, characters or music from any studio.

## Picture
- **Canvas** 1280 x 720 at 24 fps. Painted layers (sky, buildings, interiors) are drawn once per scene in SVG and rasterised at 1.6x, so they read as painted backgrounds; characters and moving things are drawn every frame on top.
- **Palette.** Night is indigo to slate (`#0a0f1c` to `#3a4560`) with sodium-warm lamplight (`#ffd98a`); the laundry interior is cream walls, mint tiles and pale green machines. Characters take a cool ambient tint outdoors at night (amount about 0.17) and none indoors.
- **Characters.** Brown line art (`#3a2622`), two-tone cel shading, large eyes with lids and blinks, blush and tears as overlays. Mio: chestnut bob, yellow star clip, mustard hoodie then red duffle coat. Itsuki: tall, messy blue-black hair, round glasses, grey cardigan then navy coat. Mrs. Hoshino: grey bun, green apron.
- **Cameras.** Mostly still. Wide shots hold on the whole laundry; close and medium-close shots push in very slowly. Cut on the listener's reaction as often as on the speaker.
- **Inserts** are full-frame: open books with underlines and pencil notes (grey for the grandfather, blue for Mio), the lined Laundry Notebook (dark pencil for Itsuki, blue for Mio), manuscripts, the dryer sign, the cat, phone screens.
- **Seasons and weather** carry time: late-summer crickets and fireflies, autumn leaves, a typhoon in October, snow from December, cherry petals in the finale.
- **Captions** are always on: sans for speech, italic serif in parentheses for thoughts, paged two lines at a time.

## Sound
- **Voices** are Kokoro: Mio `af_heart:0.75+af_sky:0.25` at 1.04, Itsuki `am_michael` at 0.93, Mrs. Hoshino `bf_emma` at 0.9, Genzo `bm_george` at 0.86, Fumiko a blend of `af_sarah` and `bf_emma` at 0.86. Thoughts are low-passed with a little room; phone calls are band-passed; memories are softened.
- **Score** is generated: an original D-major waltz for the theme (OP, bridges), a sparse night piano, a light pizzicato tease for Mio's jokes, a minor sorrow theme, a wonder celesta, a memory music-box, an unease drone, and hope. Cues are normalised to the same loudness and ducked under dialogue.
- **Ambience** never drops out: washers, crickets, rain, storm, wind, snow, city, train, birds.
- **SFX** are small and soft: the door chime, page turns, the vending machine, the can, the cat, the dryer buzzer, a pencil.

## Shape of an episode
Cold open (about 1 minute), opening titles (52 s), title card, Part A, eyecatch, Part B, the bridge, each of them alone at home, ending titles with the episode's Dostoevsky line, and a next-time card voiced by Mio.
