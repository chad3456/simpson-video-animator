logline: January, deep snow. Mio announces, very casually, that she won't apply to the university in Tokyo after all: her mother needs help with the baby, and the local college is fine. Over Ivan Karamazov's rebellion and his poem of the Grand Inquisitor, who takes away people's freedom to make them comfortable, Itsuki argues for her freedom against his own heart, and tells her to go.
