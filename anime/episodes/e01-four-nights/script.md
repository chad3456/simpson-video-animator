# Dostoevsky at the Coin Laundry E1: "Four Nights"

*Late August. In a 24-hour coin laundry by the canal, a teasing seventeen-year-old notices that the silent man at the far end of the bench is reading her late grandfather's favourite story, about a lonely dreamer who gets four nights with a girl on a bridge.*

Book: White Nights (1848). Format: anime, about 15 minutes. Original story. Discusses White Nights (1848); Dostoevsky passages are our own renderings of the public-domain texts.

Original characters, designs and music. Not affiliated with any studio.
