On the same Saturday, Mio takes the train to Tokyo for her entrance exam and Itsuki takes the local line to his mother's care home, carrying Father Zosima's story of his dying brother, who asked the birds to forgive him. That Thursday, Mrs. Hoshino tells Mio something about Itsuki's washing machine that he will never say himself.
