# Dostoevsky at the Coin Laundry E3: "Letters in the Lost-and-Found"

*Late September. For the first time, Mio isn't there when Itsuki arrives. She has left him a letter in the laundry's lost-and-found notebook, because Dostoevsky's first novel is made of letters, and because some things are easier to say on paper. He writes back.*

Book: Poor Folk (1846). Format: anime, about 15 minutes. Original story. Discusses Poor Folk (1846); Dostoevsky passages are our own renderings of the public-domain texts.

Original characters, designs and music. Not affiliated with any studio.
