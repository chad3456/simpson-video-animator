logline: Late March, the last night of the Coin Laundry Hoshizora, and the thirty-first Thursday. Mio leaves for Tokyo on Saturday. Over the last chapter of Dostoevsky's last novel, Alyosha's speech to twelve schoolboys at a stone, the two of them give each other the only presents that fit, and Itsuki finally fixes the sign.
quote: Let us never forget one another. And whatever happens, even if we never meet again, let us remember how good it once was here, all of us together.
