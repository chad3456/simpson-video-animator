# Mahabharata: Dharma Above All Bonds

A short cinematic retelling of the Mahabharata. Every frame is painted in code with p5.js and p5.brush (watercolour skies, shadow-puppet heroes with gold accents) and animated in code: camera moves, parallax, rim light, live brush effects.

Length: 4:01.9. Theme: truth and dharma above personal bonds.

## Prologue

**0:00.0**

*Black. A drop of red ink blooms into a blood-red sun.*

> धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे

> on the field of dharma, the field of the Kurus

**NARRATOR:** Long ago, on a field called Kurukshetra, one family went to war with itself.

**0:08.2**

*Kurukshetra at dawn. Two vast armies, spears like reeds, banners in the wind. A conch sounds.*

**NARRATOR:** Two armies. One bloodline. Cousins and brothers, teachers and sons, facing each other across the dust.

**0:17.7**

*A gold mandala turns on indigo. A brush stroke of red paint sweeps behind the title.*

> महाभारत

> dharma above all bonds

## The heroes

**0:25.2** · **BHISHMA** (भीष्म), *the terrible vow*

*A storm over the Ganga. Bhishma rises from the river, one hand raised to the sky as lightning seals his vow.*

**NARRATOR:** Bhishma, son of the river Ganga. He swore never to marry and never to rule, and to guard the throne until his last breath.

**0:35.7** · **YUDHISHTHIRA** (युधिष्ठिर), *son of dharma*

*A calm golden dawn. The wheel of dharma turns behind Yudhishthira, spear in hand.*

**NARRATOR:** Yudhishthira, eldest of the five Pandava brothers. Son of Dharma, the man who would not lie.

**0:44.3** · **BHIMA** (भीम), *son of the wind*

*Boulders under a violet storm. Bhima lifts his mace overhead; the wind howls past him.*

**NARRATOR:** Bhima, son of the Wind, with the strength of ten thousand elephants.

**0:50.8** · **ARJUNA** (अर्जुन), *the peerless archer*

*Teal dusk. Arjuna draws the Gandiva. A wooden bird sits on a far pole. He looses, and the arrow of light strikes its eye.*

**NARRATOR:** Arjuna, son of Indra. The archer who saw nothing but the eye of the bird.

**0:58.3** · **DRAUPADI** (द्रौपदी), *born of fire*

*Darkness and a sacrificial altar. Draupadi rises out of the flames, embers flying.*

**NARRATOR:** Draupadi, born from the sacrificial fire. Queen of the Pandavas, who would never let a wrong be forgotten.

**1:07.8** · **KARNA** (कर्ण), *son of the sun*

*A colossal sun. Karna stands before it in his golden armour, bow in hand.*

**NARRATOR:** Karna, son of the Sun, born in golden armour. The most generous of men, bound by loyalty to the wrong side.

**1:17.6** · **DURYODHANA** (दुर्योधन), *the prince who would not yield*

*A crimson storm, a serpent banner, a dark palace. Duryodhana, mace on his shoulder.*

**NARRATOR:** Duryodhana, eldest of the hundred Kaurava princes, who wanted the whole kingdom and would share none of it.

**1:26.6** · **KRISHNA** (कृष्ण), *the charioteer*

*Moonlit Vrindavan, fireflies over the Yamuna. Krishna plays the flute.*

**NARRATOR:** And Krishna. Cowherd, prince and friend. In the war to come, he swore he would not lift a single weapon.

## The fall

**1:35.5**

*The court of Hastinapura. Shakuni and Yudhishthira kneel over the board; the dice tumble again and again.*

**NARRATOR:** Then came the game of dice. Shakuni's dice could not lose. Yudhishthira staked his kingdom, his brothers, himself, and at last, Draupadi.

**1:47.5**

*The court. Draupadi raises her arm to the sky while she is dragged by her sari; the elders bow their heads. Golden light falls, and the sari spirals up without end.*

**NARRATOR:** They dragged her into the court to shame her. The elders looked away. She called out to Krishna, and her sari had no end.

**1:57.1**

*An autumn forest. Five brothers and Draupadi walk into exile as leaves fall.*

**NARRATOR:** Thirteen years of exile in the forest. When they came back, they asked for only five villages.

**2:05.4**

*Krishna stands before Duryodhana's throne as envoy of peace.*

**NARRATOR:** Krishna himself went to ask for peace. Duryodhana answered:

**DURYODHANA:** Not even as much land as the point of a needle.

**2:14.3**

*Kurukshetra. The two hosts and their chariots fill the plain; Arjuna's monkey banner flies.*

**NARRATOR:** So eighteen armies gathered at Kurukshetra. And the conches sounded.

## The Gita

**2:22.3**

*Krishna drives the white horses between the armies, Arjuna standing behind him.*

**ARJUNA:** Krishna, take my chariot between the two armies. Let me see whom I must fight.

**2:28.8**

*Arjuna sees Bhishma and Drona across the field. His bow slips from his hand.*

**ARJUNA:** My grandfather. My teacher. My own blood. How can I kill them? I will not fight, Krishna.

**2:37.8**

*Krishna turns, a halo behind him, and speaks.*

> कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन

**KRISHNA:** Arjuna. The soul is never born, and it never dies. You have a right to your action, but never to its fruits. Stand up, and do your duty.

**2:48.6**

*The sky tears open into the cosmos. Krishna's universal form: many heads, many arms holding the chakra, conch, mace and lotus, galaxies turning around him. Arjuna, tiny, bows.*

**NARRATOR:** Then Krishna showed Arjuna his true form. The light of a thousand suns at once. Every god, every world, every age, within one body.

**3:01.5**

*The cosmic faces blaze; streams of warriors pour into the fire of Time.*

> कालोऽस्मि

**KRISHNA (as Time):** I am Time, the destroyer of worlds. These warriors are already slain by me. Be only my instrument, Arjuna.

**3:11.2**

*Dawn. Arjuna lifts the Gandiva and draws.*

**NARRATOR:** And Arjuna lifted his bow.

## The war

**3:16.2**

*Sunset. Bhishma lies on a bed of arrows.*

**NARRATOR:** For eighteen days the field burned. Bhishma fell, and lay upon a bed of arrows.

**3:23.6**

*The chakravyuha: rings of soldiers wheel around young Abhimanyu, who holds up a chariot wheel.*

**NARRATOR:** Young Abhimanyu broke into the wheel formation, and never came out.

**3:29.8**

*Karna strains to lift his sinking chariot wheel. An arrow of light streaks in.*

**NARRATOR:** Karna's chariot wheel sank into the earth, and the Sun's son fell.

**3:35.9**

*Dusk by the lake. Bhima and Duryodhana clash with maces; Duryodhana falls.*

**NARRATOR:** And on the last day, by the lake, Bhima's mace brought down Duryodhana.

## Dawn

**3:42.4**

*A silent dawn over the field. Broken wheels and spears. Krishna and Arjuna stand together; the flute plays.*

**NARRATOR:** The Pandavas won. Not because the bonds of blood did not matter, but because truth mattered more.

**3:52.4**

*The gold mandala again, and the closing verse.*

> यतो धर्मस्ततो जयः

> where there is dharma, there is victory

**NARRATOR:** Yato dharmas tato jayah. Where there is dharma, there is victory.
