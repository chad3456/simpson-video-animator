# Ramayana: a pop-up storybook

A short animated retelling of the Ramayana, about 2:53 long, drawn as a flat cut-paper pop-up book in a pastel room (style study of the reference clip: open book with two coloured pages, dioramas that pop up as the page turns, diagonal colour bands, white label tags).

## 1. Ayodhya  (0:00)

**[Ayodhya, on the river Sarayu.]**  
Long ago, on the banks of the river Sarayu, stood Ayodhya, a city of gold and gardens.

**[Four princes are born.]**  
Its king, Dasharatha, prayed for sons, and four were born: Rama, Bharata, Lakshmana and Shatrughna.

## 2. Bow  (0:17)

**[The great bow of Shiva.]**  
In Mithila, King Janaka set a challenge. Whoever could string the great bow of Shiva would marry his daughter, Sita.

**[Rama breaks the bow.]**  
Prince after prince failed. Then Rama lifted it, drew it, and the bow snapped in two. Rama and Sita were married.

## 3. Exile  (0:36)

**[Two old promises.]**  
But back home, Queen Kaikeyi claimed two old promises from the king. Her son Bharata would rule, and Rama would live in the forest for fourteen years.

**[Fourteen years in the forest.]**  
Rama left without a word of anger. Sita and his brother Lakshmana went with him.

**[Sandals on the throne.]**  
Bharata refused the crown. He placed Rama's sandals on the throne, and waited.

## 4. Deer  (1:00)

**[A golden deer.]**  
Deep in the Panchavati forest, a golden deer appeared. Sita longed for it, and Rama gave chase.

**[It was a trap.]**  
But the deer was a demon in disguise, and the chase was a trap.

## 5. Abduction  (1:15)

**[Ravana of Lanka.]**  
Ravana, the ten-headed king of Lanka, seized Sita and carried her south in his flying chariot.

**[Brave Jatayu.]**  
The old vulture Jatayu flew up to stop him, fought bravely, and fell.

## 6. Hanuman  (1:30)

**[Hanuman and Sugriva.]**  
Searching for Sita, the brothers met Hanuman, and the vanara king Sugriva.

**[An army of vanaras.]**  
Sugriva called his people, and a great army of vanaras gathered to help.

## 7. Leap  (1:43)

**[One leap across the sea.]**  
Hanuman grew huge, and leapt across the ocean to Lanka.

**[Rama's ring.]**  
In the Ashoka grove he found Sita, and gave her Rama's ring, so she would know help was coming.

**[Lanka in flames.]**  
Caught by the guards, he let them set his tail alight, and leapt from roof to roof until Lanka burned.

## 8. Bridge  (2:03)

**[A bridge of floating stones.]**  
To reach Lanka, the army built a bridge across the sea, from stones that floated.

**[Every stone says Rama.]**  
Each one carried Rama's name.

## 9. Battle  (2:15)

**[The great war.]**  
The war was long and fierce.

**[One arrow.]**  
At last, Rama loosed a single arrow, and Ravana fell.

## 10. Return  (2:24)

**[Home to Ayodhya.]**  
Rama, Sita and Lakshmana flew home to Ayodhya, in the flying chariot.

**[A festival of lamps.]**  
The people lit rows and rows of lamps to light their way.

**[Rama is crowned.]**  
And Rama was crowned king.

## 11. End  (2:39)

Those lamps are still lit every year, for a story of duty, love and courage. The Ramayana.
