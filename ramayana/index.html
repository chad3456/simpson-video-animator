Ramayana in one hundred paintings

Valmiki’s epic, from the first verse to the coronation, as a hundred paintings you can lean into. Each one is drawn live in the browser as flat gouache cut-outs, then painted over by a stack of shaders: paper grain, pigment, gold leaf, running water, firelight. Move the pointer to shift the depth and carry a lantern across the sheet.

Scenes and chapter references follow Bibek Debroy’s translation of the Critical Edition. Where a familiar episode is not in Valmiki, the caption says so.
