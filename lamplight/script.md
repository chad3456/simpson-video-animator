# Lamplight: a Ramayana in shadow

Film, 7:19. Narrated by an old shadow-puppet player whose family has carried the puppets for eleven generations. The look follows Tholu Bommalata, the translucent coloured leather puppets of Andhra Pradesh, played behind a cloth by the light of one lamp. The story follows Valmiki's Ramayana, with one folk story (the squirrel at the bridge) that the puppet players always tell.

In the live version the viewer holds the lamp. The lines marked **[you]** are the moments that wait for the viewer; in the film the puppeteer does them. Shots marked *behind the screen* cut to the puppeteer's side of the cloth.

**0:49** And with that verse, he began to tell the story of Rama. This is how it goes.
