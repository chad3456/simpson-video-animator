Lamplight

A Ramayana for one lamp and a cloth screen.

An old puppeteer tells the story with leather puppets. You hold the lamp. Where you move the light, the shadows follow, and some moments wait for you.

Sound on · nine short acts, about 12 minutes · mouse, trackpad or finger

इति

The lamp is out

The story is from Valmiki's Ramayana. The look is borrowed from Tholu Bommalata, the coloured leather shadow puppets of Andhra Pradesh, which are still played behind a cloth by lamplight.
