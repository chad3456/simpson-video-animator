# Shiva Purana · Issue One: The Pillar of Fire

*Who is the greatest of the gods?*

Brahma and Vishnu go to war over who is supreme, until a column of fire with no beginning and no end splits the sky between them.

Source: Shiva Purana, Vidyeshvara Samhita, chapters 6 to 9 (tr. Bibek Debroy)

### Scene: Naimisha forest `0:09`

*Narrator:* In the forest of Naimisha, the sages gathered around Suta, the great teller of tales.

**Sage:** O Suta, tell us. Why is Shiva worshipped as the lingam, and not only in his human form?

**Suta:** That is the oldest story of all. It begins with a quarrel between two gods.

**Suta:** Listen, and I will tell you what Nandi once told the sage Sanatkumara.

## Issue One: The Pillar of Fire `0:34`

## Part One: I Am the Lord! `0:41`

### Scene: Long, long ago `0:46`

*Narrator:* Once, long ago, Vishnu lay resting on the great serpent Shesha, surrounded by splendour.

*Narrator:* Then Brahma, the creator, came wandering by on his swan.

**Brahma:** What kind of fellow are you? You see me, and you keep lying there?

**Brahma:** Get up, child! Your lord has arrived!

**Vishnu:** Welcome, child. Sit. Why does your face look so troubled?

**Brahma:** I am the grandfather of the universe. I am your protector!

**Vishnu:** The whole universe lives inside me. You were born from the lotus in my navel. You are my son.

**Brahma:** I am the lord!

**Vishnu:** No. I am the lord!

### Scene: war `1:26`

*Narrator:* They fought. Their mounts fought, the swan against the eagle Garuda. Their followers fought on both sides.

*Narrator:* The devas came in their chariots to watch. At first they showered flowers. Then they began to tremble.

**Vishnu:** Then face the Maheshvara weapon!

**Brahma:** And you, the Pashupata!

*Narrator:* The two weapons rose like ten thousand suns. The three worlds began to burn. It was as if the end of time had come.

### Scene: Mount Kailasa `1:58`

*Narrator:* Terrified, the devas fled to Mount Kailasa, where Shiva sat with Uma, surrounded by his ganas.

**Indra:** Mahadeva, save us! Brahma and Vishnu are burning down the worlds!

**Shiva:** Children, I already know about their battle. Do not be afraid.

**Shiva:** I will go there myself.

## Part Two: The Column Without End `2:18`

### Scene: pillar `2:23`

*Narrator:* Shiva hid himself in the clouds. And between the two gods, a terrible column of fire burst into being.

*Narrator:* Both blazing weapons fell into it, and were quenched in an instant.

**Vishnu:** What is this? It is beyond all the senses.

**Brahma:** It rises past the sky, and sinks below the earth.

**Vishnu:** Let us find its top and its bottom. We must do it together.

**Vishnu:** I will go down. You go up.

### Scene: search `2:53`

*Narrator:* Vishnu became a great boar and dug down into the underworld. Brahma became a swan and flew up into the sky.

*Narrator:* Down and down went the boar. Up and up flew the swan. Years passed.

### Scene: ketaki `3:08`

*Narrator:* High above, Brahma saw something drifting down. A ketaki flower, as fresh and fragrant as the day it fell.

**Brahma:** O king of flowers! Why are you falling? Who wore you?

**Ketaki Flower:** I fell from Shiva's hair when he laughed and shook his head. I have been falling for ages, and I have only reached the middle.

**Ketaki Flower:** You will never see the top.

**Brahma:** Then, my great friend, do something for me. Come with me to Vishnu.

**Brahma:** Tell him that Brahma has seen the top of the column.

*Narrator:* Brahma bowed again and again before the flower. And the flower agreed.

### Scene: lie `3:45`

*Narrator:* Vishnu returned, worn out, his joy gone. Brahma saw him and danced with delight.

**Vishnu:** I went below the underworld. I could not find where it begins.

**Brahma:** O Hari! I have seen the top of the column. And this ketaki is my witness.

**Brahma:** Tell him!

**Ketaki Flower:** It is true. He saw the top.

*Narrator:* Vishnu believed them. He bowed before Brahma and worshipped him.

## Part Three: The Truth Revealed `4:11`

### Scene: reveal `4:16`

*Narrator:* Then, to punish the deceit, Shiva himself stepped out of the lingam of fire.

**Vishnu:** Your form has no beginning and no end. It was our pride that blinded us. Forgive us, lord.

**Shiva:** Hari, I am pleased with you. Even while you longed to be lord, you did not stray from the truth.

**Shiva:** From today, you will be worshipped as my equal, with your own temples, your own festivals.

### Scene: bhairava `4:44`

*Narrator:* From between his eyebrows, Mahadeva brought forth a terrible being. Bhairava.

**Bhairava:** Lord, what is your command?

**Shiva:** This is Brahma, the first of the gods. Honour him with your sharp sword.

*Narrator:* Bhairava seized the fifth head, the one that had told the lie. Brahma shook like a plantain leaf in the wind.

**Vishnu:** O Isha! It was you who gave him these five heads. Forgive him. Restrain your anger.

**Shiva:** Bhairava. Stop.

### Scene: curse `5:17`

*Narrator:* Brahma's life was spared. From that day he had four heads. But there was still a price.

**Shiva:** Brahma, you lied for the sake of worship. So you will not be worshipped in the worlds. No temples, no festivals of your own.

**Brahma:** Lord, you have spared my head. That alone is a boon to me.

**Shiva:** Then take another. In every sacrifice and sacred rite, you will be the guru. Without you, no sacrifice will bear fruit.

### Scene: flowerjudged `5:44`

**Shiva:** And you, ketaki. You bore false witness. Go far away. I will never again be worshipped with you.

**Ketaki Flower:** Forgive me, father! I have seen you now. How can the sin of a lie remain on me?

**Shiva:** I speak the truth, so I will not wear you. But my followers will. And you may adorn the canopies above me.

### Scene: lingam `6:04`

*Narrator:* Humbled at last, Brahma and Vishnu worshipped Shiva together, the first ever to do so.

**Shiva:** This day will be called Shivaratri. Whoever fasts and worships my lingam on this night gains the fruit of a whole year of worship.

**Shiva:** The column has no beginning and no end. But so that the world may see it and worship it, it will become small.

*Narrator:* The mountain of fire became Arunachala, the red mountain. And the lingam became the sign of Shiva, formless and with form, everywhere at once.

### Scene: close `6:39`

**Suta:** So remember. Pride blinds even gods. And the one who tells the truth, like Vishnu, is honoured.

**Sage:** And who was the next to be humbled, Suta?

**Suta:** The cleverest sage of all. Narada.
