# Shiva Purana · Issue Five: Daksha's Daughter

*How Sati won Shiva, and how she tested him*

The goddess is born as Daksha's daughter Sati and wins Shiva through devotion. Then, in a forest, she doubts him, takes the form of Sita, and pays a price.

Source: Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Sati Khanda, chapters 14 to 25 (tr. Bibek Debroy)

### Scene: Naimisha forest `0:08`

**Suta:** Shiva sat alone in meditation, wanting nothing. So the gods worried. How would the world go on, if the destroyer never married?

**Suta:** So the great goddess herself agreed to be born on earth, as the daughter of Daksha.

## Issue Five: Daksha's Daughter `0:24`

## Part One: The Girl Who Drew Shiva `0:31`

### Scene: Daksha's palace `0:36`

*Narrator:* Daksha, son of Brahma and lord of all creatures, and his wife Asikni had a daughter. They named her Sati.

**Daksha:** Look at her, Asikni. She glows like the moon.

*Narrator:* As she grew, Sati played like other children. But she drew only one face, again and again. The face of Shiva.

### Scene: vow `0:56`

*Narrator:* When she was grown, she kept the Nanda vow for a whole year. She fasted, she prayed, she meditated on Shiva alone.

*Narrator:* On the ninth day of the bright fortnight, Shiva himself appeared before her. Radiant, with the moon on his crest and the river in his hair.

**Shiva:** Daughter of Daksha, I am pleased with your vow. Ask for any boon.

**Sati:** Lord, grant me the boon I desire...

**Shiva:** Be my wife.

*Narrator:* He did not even let her finish.

**Sati:** Then ask my father for my hand, and marry me by the proper rites.

## Part Two: The Wedding `1:32`

### Scene: wedding `1:37`

*Narrator:* Brahma himself carried Shiva's proposal to Daksha. And Daksha, whatever he felt in his heart, gave his daughter away.

*Narrator:* Sati and Shiva went around the sacred fire, and the gods showered flowers.

*Narrator:* Then Shiva took his bride to Kailasa on Nandi, and for ages they wandered the worlds together, happy.

## Part Three: The Test in the Forest `1:59`

### Scene: The Dandaka forest `2:04`

*Narrator:* One day, unseen, Shiva and Sati came to the Dandaka forest. There, a prince wandered, weeping, with his brother at his side.

**Rama:** Sita! Sita! Trees, birds, have you seen my Sita?

**Shiva:** Victory to you, Rama!

**Sati:** Lord, you bow to a man who weeps and wanders like a madman? The master does not bow to the servant.

**Shiva:** That is Rama, son of Dasharatha. He is Vishnu himself, born on earth. His brother Lakshmana is a part of Shesha.

**Sati:** Vishnu? Lost and crying in a forest? I do not believe it.

**Shiva:** If you do not believe me, Sati, go and test him yourself.

### Scene: test `2:43`

**Sati:** If he is truly Vishnu, he will know me. If he is only a man, he will run to his wife.

*Narrator:* She took on the form of Sita, and walked toward him.

*Narrator:* Rama looked at her, and smiled, and bowed his head. And he whispered the name of Shiva.

**Rama:** Mother Sati, I bow to you. But why are you alone in this forest? Where is Shambhu?

**Rama:** And why have you put aside your own form, and taken this one?

**Sati:** Forgive me, Rama. My husband bowed to you, and I doubted him. Now I know who you are.

**Rama:** Long ago, Shiva crowned Vishnu with his own hands and bowed to him, and told all the gods to do the same. That is why he bowed to me. His love made him do it.

### Scene: vowsilent `3:29`

**Shiva:** Did you test him?

**Sati:** I... did nothing, lord. I only looked.

*Narrator:* But Shiva saw everything in his meditation. Sati had taken the form of Sita, wife of the Lord he worshipped.

**Shiva:** In that form, she is like a mother to me. I cannot see her as my wife in this body again.

**A Voice from the Sky:** Blessed is the great lord. Who else in the three worlds could keep such a vow?

**Sati:** What vow? What has he resolved? He will not tell me... I have lost him, and I do not even know how.

*Narrator:* They went home to Kailasa. Shiva sat in meditation for eighty-seven thousand years. And a silence grew between them.

### Scene: close `4:09`

**Suta:** Even the goddess was tested by Shiva's maya, so that the world would learn. Doubt the one you love, and something breaks.

**Sage:** And did that silence ever end, Suta?

**Suta:** It ended. In fire. At her father's sacrifice.
