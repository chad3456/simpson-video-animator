# Shiva Purana · Issue Seven: Virabhadra

*The wrath of Shiva*

Shiva tears out a lock of his hair, and from it rise Virabhadra and Mahakali. Not even Vishnu can stand in their way.

Source: Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Sati Khanda, chapters 32 to 43 (tr. Bibek Debroy)

### Scene: Naimisha forest `0:08`

**Suta:** Sati was gone. Her ganas fled to Kailasa, and Narada told Shiva everything.

**Suta:** And the calmest being in the universe became the most terrible.

## Issue Seven: Virabhadra `0:20`

## Part One: A Lock of Hair `0:27`

### Scene: wrath `0:32`

**Narada:** Lord, she asked for your share, and he insulted you before everyone. So she gave up her body in yogic fire.

*Narrator:* Shiva's eyes turned red. He plucked a single lock from his matted hair, and flung it down upon the mountain.

*Narrator:* The lock split in two. From one half rose Virabhadra, tall as the sky, blazing like the fire at the end of time. From the other rose Mahakali, surrounded by a host of spirits.

**Virabhadra:** Lord, command me! Shall I dry up the oceans? Crush the mountains? Burn the gods to ash?

**Shiva:** Go to Daksha's sacrifice. Destroy it. And bring down all who stand with him.

## Part Two: The March `1:15`

### Scene: omens `1:20`

*Narrator:* At Kanakhala, the omens turned black. Jackals howled. Blood rained from the sky. The sacrificial fire sputtered.

**Daksha:** Vishnu, protect my sacrifice! You are the lord of sacrifice!

**Vishnu:** Daksha, you insulted the lord of all. Nothing can save a sacrifice that dishonours Shiva. Still, I am here. I will fight.

### Scene: battle `1:43`

*Narrator:* The ganas fell upon the gods. Indra hurled his thunderbolt, Agni his spear, Yama his staff. Gana fought god, two by two.

*Narrator:* Mahakali and her yoginis swept through the ranks of the gods like a storm.

**Vishnu:** Sudarshana!

*Narrator:* The blazing discus flew. And one of Shiva's warriors, Kshetrapala, opened his mouth and swallowed it whole.

*Narrator:* Vishnu seized him by the throat and squeezed until he spat it out again.

## Part Three: Vishnu Against Virabhadra `2:15`

### Scene: duel `2:20`

*Narrator:* Then Vishnu himself faced Virabhadra. From Vishnu's body sprang countless warriors bearing conch, discus and mace.

*Narrator:* Virabhadra remembered Shiva, raised his trident, and burned them all to ash.

*Narrator:* His trident struck Vishnu on the chest, and Vishnu fell senseless to the ground.

*Narrator:* He rose again, eyes red, and raised the discus. But by Shiva's will, it froze in his hand and would not move.

**Virabhadra:** Stand there like a mountain, Vishnu!

*Narrator:* Vishnu broke free and drew his great bow. Virabhadra cut it into three pieces with three arrows.

**Vishnu:** This is what Sati's sacrifice has set in motion. No one can resist it. Let Shiva's will be done.

### Scene: fall `3:08`

*Narrator:* Vishnu withdrew. Brahma withdrew. The sacrifice itself took the form of a deer and fled into the sky, and Virabhadra cut it down.

**Daksha:** No! Mercy!

*Narrator:* Virabhadra struck off Daksha's head, and cast it into the sacrificial fire. Then he and his ganas returned to Kailasa.

## Part Four: The Goat-Headed King `3:30`

### Scene: plea `3:35`

*Narrator:* Brahma and Vishnu came to Kailasa with the gods, and praised Shiva, and begged for mercy.

**Brahma:** Lord, Daksha was a fool, and he has been punished. But without the sacrifice, the world cannot go on. Restore it. Forgive him.

**Shiva:** I do not hold this against children. Daksha's head has burned in the fire. Let him wear the head of a goat.

### Scene: revive `3:59`

*Narrator:* Shiva looked upon the ruined sacrifice with kindness, and everyone who had fallen rose again, whole. And Daksha rose too, with the head of a goat.

**Daksha:** Lord, I insulted you, and you have given me life. I was blind with pride. You are the lord of all. Forgive me.

**Shiva:** Complete your sacrifice, Daksha. And this time, remember whose share it is.

*Narrator:* And so the great sacrifice was finished, and Shiva received his share. But Sati was gone.

### Scene: alone `4:29`

*Narrator:* Shiva returned to the Himalaya, and sank into the deepest meditation, alone.

### Scene: close `4:37`

**Suta:** Even in his greatest anger, Shiva gave back what he took. That is the difference between his wrath and ours.

**Sage:** But Sati? Did she never come back?

**Suta:** She did. As the daughter of the mountain. Parvati.
