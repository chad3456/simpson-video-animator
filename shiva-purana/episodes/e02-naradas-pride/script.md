# Shiva Purana · Issue Two: Narada's Pride

*The sage who wanted to marry a princess*

Narada defeats desire, and then boasts about it. So Vishnu builds him a city, a princess and a very surprising face.

Source: Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Srishti Khanda, chapters 2 to 4 (tr. Bibek Debroy)

### Scene: Naimisha forest `0:08`

**Suta:** Narada is the wisest of sages. He wanders the three worlds singing Vishnu's name.

**Suta:** And yet, once, even Narada was fooled. Not by a demon. By himself.

## Issue Two: Narada's Pride `0:21`

## Part One: The Sage Who Beat Desire `0:28`

### Scene: A cave in the Himalaya `0:33`

*Narrator:* Beside the heavenly river, in a beautiful Himalayan cave, Narada sat still and silent, deep in meditation.

*Narrator:* His austerities grew so strong that, far away in heaven, Indra began to worry.

### Scene: Indra's court `0:49`

**Indra:** That sage wants my throne, Kama. I am sure of it. He will ask Brahma for my kingdom.

**Indra:** You have broken the pride of many ascetics. Go and break his.

**Kama:** With Vasanta and his spring beside me, no sage can resist.

### Scene: attack `1:03`

*Narrator:* Spring burst over the mountain. Flowers bloomed, cuckoos sang, and Kama drew his flowered bow.

*Narrator:* Nothing happened. Narada did not even open his eyes.

**Kama:** My arrows... they have no power here!

*Narrator:* For this was the very spot where Shiva had once burned Kama to ashes, and Shiva had declared that Kama's tricks would never work here.

*Narrator:* Narada knew none of this. When he opened his eyes, he believed he had beaten desire all by himself.

**Narada:** I have conquered Kama himself!

### Scene: Mount Kailasa `1:38`

**Narada:** Mahadeva! Kama came to tempt me, with spring itself. And I did not move! I defeated him!

**Shiva:** You are blessed, Narada. But listen to me. Do not tell this to anyone else.

**Shiva:** Above all, do not tell it to Vishnu. Keep it a secret. I say this because I love you.

*Narrator:* But Narada was already lost in Shiva's maya. He heard the words, and did not listen.

### Scene: Vaikuntha `2:02`

*Narrator:* He went to Brahma and boasted. Then he went straight to Vishnu.

**Narada:** Lord, I sat in the Himalaya, and Kama himself could not shake me!

**Vishnu:** Mahadeva, your maya is blessed. It fools everyone.

**Vishnu:** Desire troubles only those without devotion. How could it ever touch you?

**Narada:** As long as you are kind to me, lord, what power has Kama over me?

## Part Two: A City Made of Maya `2:25`

### Scene: city `2:30`

*Narrator:* On Narada's road, Vishnu raised a city out of nothing. A hundred yojanas wide, more beautiful than heaven.

*Narrator:* There, King Shilanidhi was holding a svayamvara for his daughter. Princes had come from every direction.

### Scene: King Shilanidhi's palace `2:47`

**King Shilanidhi:** Great sage, this is my daughter, Shrimati. Tell me her fortune. What kind of husband will she have?

*Narrator:* Narada looked at the princess. And the sage who had beaten desire forgot everything.

**Narada:** Her husband will be a lord of everything. Unconquerable. Like Shiva. One who has conquered Kama!

**Narada:** One who has conquered Kama... that is me! But will she choose me, among all these princes?

**Narada:** Women love beauty. And who is more beautiful than Vishnu?

### Scene: ask `3:15`

**Narada:** Lord, I want to marry Shrimati. Give me your form, so that she places the garland around my neck!

**Vishnu:** Go, sage. I will do what is good for you. Just as a physician treats a patient.

*Narrator:* And Vishnu gave Narada... the face of a monkey. Then he vanished.

## Part Three: The Svayamvara `3:34`

### Scene: hall `3:39`

*Narrator:* Narada sat proudly in the hall, sure he looked like Vishnu. Everyone else saw only the old Narada.

*Narrator:* But two of Shiva's ganas, disguised as brahmins, could see the truth.

**Gana:** Look at Narada! Vishnu's body, and a monkey's face!

**Gana:** And he hopes to win the princess! Ha ha ha!

**Narada:** Let them laugh. They are jealous.

### Scene: garland `4:00`

*Narrator:* Shrimati entered with a golden garland, searching the hall for her husband.

**Shrimati:** A monkey's face?

*Narrator:* She turned away in disgust. Narada was stunned.

*Narrator:* Then a new king appeared in the hall. It was Vishnu himself, in disguise.

*Narrator:* Her face bloomed like a lotus. She placed the garland around his neck, and the two of them vanished.

### Scene: mirror `4:25`

**Gana:** Narada, your desire was wasted. Look at your own face. It is a monkey's!

**Narada:** You laughed at me! Then be born as brahmins, with the bodies of rakshasas!

*Narrator:* The ganas did not curse him back. They knew he was not himself. They went away quietly, praising Shiva.

## Part Four: The Curse on Vishnu `4:43`

### Scene: curse `4:49`

**Narada:** Hari, you deceiver! You trick the whole world! You took Mohini's form and cheated the asuras of the nectar!

**Narada:** You tricked me over a woman. So in a human body you will suffer. You will lose your wife, and only monkeys will help you!

**Vishnu:** I accept your curse, sage. It comes from Shiva's will.

*Narrator:* And in that moment, Shiva lifted the maya. Narada's mind cleared, like a sky after rain.

**Narada:** Lord, what have I done? I spoke wicked words. I cursed you! Let the curse be false!

**Vishnu:** Do not grieve. It was Shiva's play, to break your pride. The curse will come true, and it will be for the good of the world.

**Vishnu:** Go to Kashi. Sing the names of Shiva, and your heart will be at peace.

*Narrator:* Ages later, Vishnu was born as Rama. He lost Sita, and an army of monkeys helped him win her back.

### Scene: forgiven `5:41`

**Gana:** Sage, we only laughed. Will you free us from your curse?

**Narada:** A curse cannot be taken back. You will be born as mighty rakshasa kings. But you will die by Vishnu's own hand, and you will be free.

### Scene: close `5:54`

**Suta:** So, the moment you think you have beaten desire, that is when it has beaten you.

**Sage:** Tell us another, Suta! Is there a story of Shiva's mercy?

**Suta:** There is. It is about a thief.
