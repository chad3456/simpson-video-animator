# Shiva Purana · Issue Ten: The Wedding of Shiva

*The strangest groom the world has ever seen*

Mena waits on the palace roof to see her famous son-in-law. Every god who arrives is more dazzling than the last, and none of them is Shiva. Then Shiva arrives.

Source: Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Parvati Khanda, chapters 43 to 55 (tr. Bibek Debroy)

### Scene: Naimisha forest `0:08`

**Suta:** The seven sages carried the proposal. The day was fixed. And from every corner of the universe, the wedding guests set out for the Himalaya.

## Issue Ten: The Wedding of Shiva `0:19`

## Part One: Is That Him? `0:26`

### Scene: The roof of Himavan's palace `0:31`

**Mena:** Narada, I must see him first. What kind of man is he, that my daughter did such tapas for him?

*Narrator:* First came the gandharvas, singing, in sparkling clothes, and the radiant Vasus.

**Mena:** Is that him? That must be Shiva!

**Narada:** No. Those are only his attendants.

*Narrator:* Then came Agni, twice as bright. Then Yama, then Varuna, then Vayu, then Kubera, each brighter than the last.

**Mena:** That one! Surely that is Rudra!

**Narada:** No.

*Narrator:* Indra, lord of the gods. Then the Moon. Then the Sun. Then Brahma himself, praised by the sages.

**Narada:** No, no, and no.

*Narrator:* And then came Vishnu, dark as a rain cloud, in yellow silk, with conch and discus, more beautiful than a crore of Kamas.

**Mena:** That is Shiva! There can be no doubt!

**Narada:** That is Vishnu, who is loved by Shiva. Shiva is greater still. I cannot even describe him.

**Mena:** If all these are his servants, how fortunate my daughter is!

### Scene: ganas `1:31`

*Narrator:* And then came Shiva's own army. Ghosts and goblins. Some with no heads, some with many heads. Some riding backwards, some blowing horns, beating drums, howling.

**Narada:** Look, Mena. There is Shiva.

*Narrator:* Ash-smeared, wearing a tiger skin, snakes coiled around his neck, a skull in his hand, riding a bull.

*Narrator:* Mena fell to the ground in a faint, like a creeper struck by the wind.

## Part Two: Mena's Rage `1:57`

### Scene: refuse `2:02`

**Mena:** I have been deceived! Narada, you wicked sage, you and your prophecies! I will not give my daughter to that ghost!

**Mena:** I would rather take her and throw myself off this mountain!

**Parvati:** Mother, I chose him with my whole heart, by my own tapas. I will marry Shiva, or no one at all.

**Vishnu:** Mena, you are seeing only one of his forms. He is beyond all forms. And he can take any form he likes.

### Scene: beautiful `2:30`

*Narrator:* So Shiva, for Mena's sake, took his most beautiful form. Fair as the moon, in silk and jewels, with a crown of crescent moon, smiling.

**Mena:** Forgive me, lord. I did not know you. My daughter is blessed. I am blessed.

## Part Three: The Wedding `2:47`

### Scene: wedding `2:52`

*Narrator:* And so the wedding was held. Himavan gave his daughter away, and Brahma himself was the priest.

*Narrator:* Parvati placed the garland around Shiva's neck, and they walked around the sacred fire together.

*Narrator:* The gods sang, the apsaras danced, and the ganas danced worst of all, and loudest.

### Scene: kama `3:13`

**Rati:** Lord, today everyone is happy except me. My husband burned so that this wedding could happen. Give him back to me.

**Shiva:** So be it, Rati.

*Narrator:* With a single glance, Kama rose from his own ashes, as handsome as before.

**Kama:** Lord, I bow to you. I shot at you, and you have given me back my life.

### Scene: Kailasa `3:35`

*Narrator:* Then Shiva and Parvati returned to Kailasa, with Nandi and all the ganas. The goddess who had been Sati was by Shiva's side once more.

*Narrator:* In time, their son Kartikeya would be born, and he would destroy the asura Taraka. But that is a story for another day.

### Scene: close `3:55`

**Suta:** A pillar of fire with no end. A proud sage with a monkey's face. A thief with a lamp. A goddess who gave her life, and came back.

**Suta:** Whoever hears these stories with love, Shiva is pleased with them. That is the Shiva Purana.

**Sage:** Om Namah Shivaya.
