# Shiva Purana · Issue Eight: The Burning of Kama

*The god of love meets the third eye*

Sati is reborn as Parvati, an asura can only be killed by Shiva's son, and Shiva will not open his eyes. So the gods send the god of love.

Source: Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Parvati Khanda, chapters 6 to 19 (tr. Bibek Debroy)

### Scene: Naimisha forest `0:08`

**Suta:** On the mountain king Himavan and his queen Mena, the goddess bestowed a gift. She was born again, as their daughter.

## Issue Eight: The Burning of Kama `0:18`

## Part One: The Daughter of the Mountain `0:25`

### Scene: The palace of Himavan `0:30`

*Narrator:* A daughter was born to Mena and Himavan, dark and glowing like a blue lotus. They named her Parvati, daughter of the mountain. Some called her Kali, some Uma.

*Narrator:* When she was a girl, Narada came to visit, and Himavan asked him to read her palm.

**Narada:** Her husband will be a yogi. He will have no mother and no father. He wears no fine clothes, and lives without any home.

**Mena:** What sort of husband is that for my daughter?

**Narada:** Do not grieve. Every sign I named belongs to Shiva. She will be his wife, as she was before.

*Narrator:* And from that day, Parvati's heart belonged to Shiva.

### Scene: serve `1:07`

*Narrator:* Shiva had come to the Himalaya to meditate. With her father's leave, Parvati served him every day. She brought flowers, water and fruit.

*Narrator:* Shiva never opened his eyes.

## Part Two: Taraka `1:20`

### Scene: taraka `1:26`

*Narrator:* Meanwhile, an asura named Taraka did terrible austerities, and asked Brahma for a boon.

**Taraka:** Let no one be able to kill me. No one, except a son born of Shiva.

**Taraka:** And Shiva is a yogi who will never marry! I am immortal!

*Narrator:* Taraka conquered the three worlds. He drove the gods out of heaven, and took their wealth and their places.

### Scene: The exiled gods `1:49`

**Indra:** Only Shiva's son can kill Taraka. And Shiva sits in meditation, and will not even look at Parvati.

**Indra:** Kama, you are our only hope. Wake desire in Shiva's heart.

**Rati:** My lord, he is Shiva. The destroyer. Please, do not go.

**Kama:** I have never failed, Rati. Come, Vasanta. Let us make it spring.

## Part Three: Spring Out of Season `2:08`

### Scene: spring `2:13`

*Narrator:* Suddenly, out of season, spring came to the frozen mountain. Trees burst into flower. Bees hummed. Cool fragrant winds began to blow.

*Narrator:* Kama hid among the trees and drew his bow of sugarcane, strung with bees.

*Narrator:* For a moment, just a moment, Shiva's mind stirred. He opened his eyes and saw Parvati.

**Shiva:** I was deep in meditation. Who has done this? Who has disturbed my mind?

*Narrator:* He looked around, and saw Kama on his left, bow drawn, about to shoot again.

### Scene: eye `2:47`

*Narrator:* Fire leapt from Shiva's third eye. Before the gods could even cry out for mercy, Kama was ash.

*Narrator:* Then Shiva vanished from the mountain. Parvati, pale and shaken, went home with her friends.

### Scene: rati `3:06`

**Rati:** What have the gods done? They called my husband here, and he burned for them! Kama, you were dearer to me than my own life!

**Indra:** Rati, keep his ashes safe. Shiva never causes sorrow without reason. He will bring your husband back.

**Shiva:** What my anger has done cannot be undone. Kama will live without a body, Ananga. And when Krishna is born on earth, Kama will be born again, as his son.

*Narrator:* And so love lives on in the world, everywhere, and invisible.

### Scene: resolve `3:36`

**Parvati:** My beauty did not win him. My service did not win him. Then I will win him the way he knows best.

**Parvati:** With tapas.

### Scene: close `3:45`

**Suta:** Desire could not move Shiva. Only devotion could.

**Suta:** And Parvati's devotion would shake the three worlds.
