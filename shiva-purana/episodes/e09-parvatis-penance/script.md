# Shiva Purana · Issue Nine: Parvati's Penance

*The austerity that won the unwinnable*

Parvati gives up her silks for bark and her food for leaves, then even leaves. Seven sages try to talk her out of it, and then a strange young ascetic tries harder.

Source: Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Parvati Khanda, chapters 22 to 30 (tr. Bibek Debroy)

### Scene: Naimisha forest `0:08`

**Suta:** Kama was ash. Shiva was gone. And Parvati made up her mind.

## Issue Nine: Parvati's Penance `0:14`

## Part One: O, Do Not! `0:21`

### Scene: The palace of Himavan `0:26`

**Parvati:** Mother, Father has agreed. Tomorrow morning I will go to the forest and do tapas, until I win Shiva.

**Mena:** Your body is so delicate, child. Do your tapas here at home. All the gods are here, all the holy places. U... ma! Oh, do not go!

*Narrator:* "U ma." Oh, do not. And that is how she came to be called Uma.

*Narrator:* But Mena saw how her daughter suffered, and at last she let her go.

### Scene: Gaurishikhara `0:54`

*Narrator:* She put away her jewels and silks, and wore bark and deerskin. She went to the very slope where Shiva had burned Kama.

*Narrator:* In summer she sat between blazing fires under the burning sun.

*Narrator:* In winter she stood in icy water through the night.

*Narrator:* First she ate fruit. Then only leaves. Then she gave up even leaves. So the world called her Aparna, the one without even a leaf.

*Narrator:* Her tapas burned so fiercely that the three worlds grew hot, and the gods ran to Shiva.

## Part Two: The Seven Sages `1:29`

### Scene: sages `1:34`

*Narrator:* Shiva sent the seven great sages to test her.

**Vasishtha:** Daughter, why torment yourself for Shiva? He is a wandering beggar. He burned Kama for nothing. He left Sati to die.

**Vasishtha:** Marry Vishnu instead. He is handsome, he is kind, he lives in Vaikuntha.

**Parvati:** Sages, you say what you believe. But I will marry Shiva, or no one. Even if he refuses me, I will do tapas for a thousand lives.

**Vasishtha:** Then you are the mother of the universe, and we bow to you.

## Part Three: The Young Ascetic `2:06`

### Scene: jatila `2:11`

*Narrator:* Then one day, a young brahmachari came by, with matted hair and a staff, glowing with his own tapas.

**The Young Ascetic:** Such hard tapas, so young! What do you want, lady? Heaven? Wealth? A husband?

**Vijaya:** She wants Shiva for her husband.

**The Young Ascetic:** Shiva? Ha! He has no family, no house, no clothes. He rides a bull and smears himself with ash from the cremation ground.

**The Young Ascetic:** Snakes around his neck. Ghosts for friends. Imagine the wedding! You in silk, and him in a tiger skin.

**Parvati:** Enough! You know nothing of Shiva. He is beyond qualities. The gods themselves wear the ash you mock!

**Parvati:** Whoever hears Shiva insulted shares the sin. Vijaya, come. I will not stay here another moment.

### Scene: reveal `2:54`

**Shiva:** Where will you go, leaving me behind, Parvati?

*Narrator:* The young ascetic was gone. Shiva himself stood in his place, smiling.

**Shiva:** I tested you, and you did not waver. I am yours, bought by your tapas. Ask me for anything.

**Parvati:** Then ask my father for my hand, lord. Let the world see you marry me properly.

**Shiva:** So be it.

### Scene: home `3:20`

*Narrator:* Parvati came home in triumph, and the whole mountain kingdom celebrated.

*Narrator:* But not everyone was happy. Shiva wanted to test Mena's heart, too.

### Scene: close `3:31`

**Suta:** The one who could not be moved by beauty or by desire, was won by steadfastness.

**Sage:** And the wedding, Suta! Tell us about the wedding!

**Suta:** Ah. The wedding. That was where Mena fainted.
