# Shiva Purana · Issue Six: The Uninvited

*Daksha's great sacrifice, and the guest he refused*

Daksha holds the greatest sacrifice the world has seen and invites everyone except Shiva. Sati goes anyway.

Source: Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Sati Khanda, chapters 26 to 31 (tr. Bibek Debroy)

### Scene: Naimisha forest `0:08`

**Suta:** Daksha never loved his son-in-law. To him, Shiva was a beggar who wore ash and skulls and lived among ghosts.

**Suta:** It began with a small thing. Shiva did not stand up.

## Issue Six: The Uninvited `0:21`

## Part One: The Insult at Prayaga `0:28`

### Scene: A gathering of sages at Prayaga `0:33`

*Narrator:* When Daksha entered the great assembly, every sage and god rose to honour him. Every one, except Brahma, his father, and Shiva.

**Daksha:** Look at him! My son-in-law, who should be like my son, does not even rise for me!

**Daksha:** He wanders burning grounds with ghosts, wears ash, and has no manners. He shall have no share in any sacrifice!

*Narrator:* Shiva said nothing. He sat as still as a mountain.

**Nandi:** You curse my lord? Then hear me! You and those who follow you will lose all true knowledge. And you, Daksha, will wear the face of a goat!

**Shiva:** Nandi. Enough. Anger is not wisdom. Come.

## Part Two: Everyone But Shiva `1:11`

### Scene: Kanakhala, Daksha's great sacrifice `1:16`

*Narrator:* Daksha began the greatest sacrifice the world had ever seen, at Kanakhala. Bhrigu led the priests. Vishnu came, Brahma came, Indra and all the gods came.

*Narrator:* Everyone was invited. Except Shiva. And except Sati.

**Dadhichi:** Daksha! Where is Shiva? Without him, this sacrifice is a corpse. It will end in ruin!

**Daksha:** I have Vishnu, the lord of sacrifice. What do I need with that wanderer?

**Dadhichi:** Then I will not stay to watch it burn.

*Narrator:* The old sage walked out. A few others followed him. Daksha laughed.

### Scene: Mount Kailasa `1:51`

*Narrator:* On Kailasa, Sati saw the Moon and his wives flying past in their chariot, all dressed in their finest.

**Sati:** Vijaya, where is everyone going?

**Vijaya:** To your father's great sacrifice, my lady. Did you not know?

### Scene: ask `2:07`

**Sati:** Lord, my father holds a great sacrifice, and all my sisters are going. Let us go too.

**Shiva:** We were not invited, Sati. To go uninvited to a house that hates you is worse than death.

**Sati:** He is my father! A daughter needs no invitation to her father's house. And I must ask him why he has insulted you.

**Shiva:** Then go, if you must. Take Nandi, and my ganas.

## Part Three: The Empty Seat `2:31`

### Scene: arrival `2:36`

*Narrator:* Sati arrived on Nandi. Her mother and sisters embraced her. But her father turned his face away.

*Narrator:* Then she saw the offerings. A share for Vishnu. A share for Brahma. A share for every god. And no share for Shiva.

**Sati:** Father! Where is the share of Shiva? Without him, how can this be a sacrifice at all?

**Daksha:** Why did you come uninvited? Your husband is a lord of ghosts, an inauspicious beggar. He gets no share here.

**Sati:** Vishnu, Brahma, you sit here and listen to this? The one you worship is insulted, and you are silent?

**Sati:** This body came from you, Daksha. I am ashamed of it. I will not keep it a moment longer.

### Scene: yogafire `3:17`

*Narrator:* She sat down on the ground and closed her eyes. She thought of only one thing. Her husband's feet.

*Narrator:* She raised her breath through her body, and a fire rose from within her. And Sati was gone.

### Scene: ribhus `3:36`

**Shiva's Ganas:** She is dead! Kill Daksha! Destroy the sacrifice!

*Narrator:* Bhrigu poured an offering into the fire, chanting from the Yajur Veda. And from the flames rose an army of fiery warriors, the Ribhus.

*Narrator:* Shiva's ganas were driven away. Many had already died of grief beside Sati.

**A Voice from the Sky:** Daksha, you fool! You did not honour Sati, the mother of the worlds, nor Shiva, the father. Your sacrifice will be destroyed. Not even the gods can save you now.

*Narrator:* And far away, on Kailasa, Narada went to tell Shiva what had happened.

### Scene: close `4:12`

**Suta:** Pride makes us cruel even to our own children. Daksha had everything, and he threw it all into the fire.

**Sage:** And Shiva? What did Shiva do?

**Suta:** He tore a lock from his hair.
