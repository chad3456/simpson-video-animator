# Shiva Purana · Issue Three: The Thief and the Lamp

*A gambler, a temple, and the night of Shiva*

A gambler steals food from a temple on Shivaratri and tears his cloth to brighten a lamp. That one flame carries him across three lives, all the way to Kailasa.

Source: Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Srishti Khanda, chapters 17 to 19 (tr. Bibek Debroy)

### Scene: Naimisha forest `0:08`

**Sage:** Suta, how did Shiva come to live on Kailasa, beside Kubera, the lord of wealth?

**Suta:** Because of a lamp. And a thief who never meant to light it.

## Issue Three: The Thief and the Lamp `0:19`

## Part One: The Worst Gambler on Earth `0:26`

### Scene: The city of Kampilya `0:31`

*Narrator:* In Kampilya lived a learned priest, Yajnadatta, honoured by the king. He had one son, Gunanidhi, handsome as the moon.

*Narrator:* But Gunanidhi had a secret. He gambled. And he lost. Every single day.

**Yajnadatta:** Where is our son? I never see him at home.

**Mother:** He has just gone out to study, with two or three friends.

*Narrator:* She loved her only son, so she lied, again and again. And Gunanidhi carried off pots, clothes and jewels to the gambling dens.

### Scene: den `1:02`

**Gambler:** Your mother's garment, last week. And today?

**Gunanidhi:** My father's ring! Set with jewels! Roll!

*Narrator:* He lost. There was no worse gambler on earth.

### Scene: ring `1:14`

**Yajnadatta:** That is my ring! Where did you get it, thief?

**Gambler:** Thief? Your son gave it to me, priest. He has given us half your house. Did you not know?

*Narrator:* Yajnadatta covered his head in shame and walked home.

### Scene: disown `1:27`

**Yajnadatta:** You hid it from me all these years! A son like that is worse than no son at all!

**Yajnadatta:** Bring me water and sesame. I will offer the rites for the dead. From today, I have no son.

### Scene: tree `1:39`

*Narrator:* Gunanidhi heard, and fled the city. He had no learning, no money, no friends.

**Gunanidhi:** Where will I go? Who will feed me now? Every morning, my mother gave me sweet food.

*Narrator:* He sat under a tree, hungry, as the sun went down. It was the night of Shivaratri.

## Part Two: One Night, One Lamp `1:57`

### Scene: temple `2:02`

*Narrator:* A devotee of Shiva passed by with his family, carrying offerings of food to the temple. They had fasted all day.

*Narrator:* All night they sang Shiva's names and worshipped the lingam.

**Gunanidhi:** That food smells wonderful. When they fall asleep, it is mine.

*Narrator:* So he sat at the temple door, without food, listening to Shiva's names all night long.

### Scene: wick `2:26`

*Narrator:* When all were asleep, he crept inside. The lamp was dying. He could barely see the food.

*Narrator:* So he tore a strip from his own cloth and twisted it into a new wick.

*Narrator:* The flame leapt up, and its light fell full upon the lingam.

*Narrator:* He grabbed the food and ran.

### Scene: chase `2:48`

**Guard:** Thief! Stop him! Seize him!

*Narrator:* The city guards caught him and killed him. He never ate a single bite of the stolen food.

## Part Three: Yama's Men and Shiva's Men `2:58`

### Scene: tug `3:03`

**Yama's Messenger:** This one is ours! He comes to Yama's city, bound in our nooses!

**Shiva's Messenger:** Release him. He is devoted to dharma. All his sins have been burned away.

**Yama's Messenger:** Devoted? He gambled, lied, stole, and even stole the food offered to Shiva!

**Shiva's Messenger:** Shiva's dharma is subtle. You see only the gross. Listen.

**Shiva's Messenger:** When the lingam was in shadow, he tore his own cloth to make a wick. He fasted all night. He heard Shiva's names, and watched his worship.

**Shiva's Messenger:** It was the night of Shivaratri. He comes with us.

*Narrator:* Yama's messengers bowed and left. And Yama told them: never bring anyone who wears Shiva's ash or rudraksha.

### Scene: Many years later, in Kalinga `3:44`

*Narrator:* After a time in Shiva's world, he was born again, as Dama, prince of Kalinga. Even as a boy, he loved Shiva.

**King Dama:** Let a lamp burn in every Shiva temple in my kingdom. Every single one. Every night.

*Narrator:* And so they did. King Dama lit a thousand lamps, for the one he had lit by accident.

## Part Four: The Lord of Wealth `4:05`

### Scene: Kashi, in another age `4:10`

*Narrator:* In another age, the same soul came to Kashi, and meditated for so long that he became skin and bone.

**Shiva:** Open your eyes, my devotee. I am pleased. Ask for a boon.

**Kubera:** Who is this lady beside Shiva? What austerities did she do, to sit so close to him?

*Narrator:* He stared, and stared again. And his left eye burst.

**Uma:** Why does this ascetic keep staring at me?

**Shiva:** Uma, he is your son. He looks at you with a child's wonder, not with wicked eyes.

**Shiva:** Be Kubera, lord of all treasure. Rule Alaka. Since your eye is yellow, they will call you Ekapinga. And I will be your friend.

**Shiva:** I will make my home on Kailasa, right beside your city.

### Scene: close `4:57`

**Suta:** A thief, a fast he never chose, and one torn piece of cloth. Even the smallest thing done for Shiva bears fruit.

**Sage:** And is there a story of a woman saved by Shiva's tale?

**Suta:** There is. Her name was Chanchula.
