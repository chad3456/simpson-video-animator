# KINDLETON Season 1: "Suicidal Empathy"

An adult (TV-MA L) animated satire season that walks through Gad Saad's *Suicidal Empathy: Dying to Be Kind* (2026) one chapter per episode. Each episode follows the same structure:

1. A cold open at the KNN news desk, where Ken's grin keeps cracking.
2. A Kindleton absurdity that turns the chapter's idea into something you can see.
3. Dr. Hal Bounds at the chalkboard, laying out the book's case in plain words and crediting Saad by name.
4. Escalation.
5. "Heart vs Head," where Dr. Iris Kindt makes the critics' strongest points and the two experts land on a usable test.
6. Penny fixes things using that test.
7. A tag that overcorrects or calls back to an earlier gag.

The jokes go after ideas, institutions, incentives and the characters themselves. The book's most charged material (immigration, Islam, grooming gangs, gender and sport, the subway case) is named and argued on screen by the two experts, never acted out by stand-ins for real groups.

## Running arcs

- **The Empathy Dial**: cranked to 11 in E1, on trial in E8, and set to 5. The Mayor sneaks it to 6 in the last shot.
- **Rocco**: freeloader, then "justice-involved raccoon", Grimsby fan, kidney donor, tax collector and finally the star witness. He never learns, and he gets a Lexus.
- **Dale's arc**: from "can't say no", through cancelled by the internet, a hairworm in the brain, Masculinity Unlearning and losing three organs to Dr. Vibes, to finally opening a jar.
- **Penny's three questions** (Is it true? Who pays? What happens next year?) come back in every resolution and become the closing argument in E8.

## Sources

Episodes 2 to 8 were punched up from the book itself: chapter titles (E4 and E7 use Saad's own), his examples and a few short quotes such as "Unicornia", "surprise house guests" and "that's the way I was meant to go". Everything else is paraphrase in the characters' own words. No book text is stored in this repo. The critics' side draws on reviews in The Bulwark and Quillette.
