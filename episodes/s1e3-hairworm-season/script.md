# KINDLETON S1E3: "Hairworm Season"

*Kindleton assumes its brutal softball rivals secretly want participation trophies too, and Dale starts cheering for the other team so hard he jumps in the town fountain.*

Format: drama-skit with explainer cutaways. Source: Ideas from 'Suicidal Empathy: Dying to Be Kind' by Gad Saad (2026), plus published criticism of the book (The Bulwark, Quillette).

Parody and commentary for adults. Strong language. Original characters. Not affiliated with any author, network or show.

- **Ken Brightside**: News anchor whose contractual grin hides a seething void (voice: `am_michael`)

- **Dale Doormat**: Dad who physically cannot say no (voice: `am_puck`)
- **Penny Doormat**: 12-year-old skeptic and the only adult in the house (voice: `af_nova`)

- **Dr. Iris Kindt**: Compassion researcher; carries the critics' counterpoint (voice: `bf_emma`)
