# KINDLETON S1E1: "Dying to Be Kind"

*When Mayor Hart cranks the town's Empathy Dial to eleven, Penny Doormat has to save her too-nice dad, her fridge and her whole town by learning when kindness helps and when it backfires.*

Format: drama-skit with explainer cutaways. Source: Ideas from 'Suicidal Empathy: Dying to Be Kind' by Gad Saad (2026), plus published criticism of the book (The Bulwark, Quillette).

Parody and commentary. Original characters. Not affiliated with any author, network or show.

## Cast

- **Ken Brightside**: News anchor, the exposition machine (voice: `am_michael`)
- **Mayor Marigold Hart**: Feelings-first mayor; if it feels good, it's law (voice: `af_sarah`)
- **Dale Doormat**: Dad who cannot say no (voice: `am_puck`)
- **Penny Doormat**: 10-year-old skeptic; asks the questions the audience is thinking (voice: `af_nova`)
- **Dr. Hal Bounds**: Evolutionary behavioral scientist; carries the book's thesis (voice: `bm_george`)
- **Dr. Iris Kindt**: Compassion researcher; carries the critics' counterpoint (voice: `bf_emma`)
- **Rocco**: A raccoon who has never heard the word no (sound effects only)

### [] INT/EXT. KNN STUDIO B, 'HEART VS HEAD'

_Dr. Hal Bounds, Ken Brightside, Dr. Iris Kindt._

**KEN BRIGHTSIDE** (happy): Tonight on Heart versus Head: is empathy killing us? On my left, Doctor Hal Bounds. On my right, compassion researcher Doctor Iris Kindt.  
<sub>ACTION: armsup; CAM: wide</sub>

**DR. IRIS KINDT**: Hal, empathy built hospitals, helped end slavery, and feeds strangers every day. Too much kindness is hardly the West's biggest problem.  
<sub>ACTION: point</sub>

**DR. HAL BOUNDS**: Agreed. Kindness isn't the disease. Uncalibrated kindness is. Feeling more for the arsonist than for the people in the burning building.  
<sub>ACTION: shrug</sub>
