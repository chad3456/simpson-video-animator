Suicidal Empathy Meter

Editorial index, not a measurement of people. Each colour shows how much a country's own news media is covering five debates, using public GDELT data. High coverage can mean a country's press is criticising a policy as much as backing it. The indicators, weights, caveats and the article lists behind every number are on this page.
