Green flag: the friend who's actually happy for you ✅🎉

When Dasharatha asked the people of Ayodhya about Rama, they said he grieves deeply when anyone is in trouble, and at every festival he rejoices like a father.

“उत्सवेषु च सर्वेषु पितेव परितुष्यति । सत्यवादी महेष्वासो वृद्धसेवी जितेन्द्रियः ॥”
Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda 2.41 (with 2.40)

Their pain, your pain. Their win, your win. Day 11 of learning from Rama.

Verse: Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda 2.41 (check it at valmiki.iitk.ac.in). English rendering ours: At every festival he rejoices like a father. He speaks the truth, is a great archer, serves his elders, and masters his senses.
