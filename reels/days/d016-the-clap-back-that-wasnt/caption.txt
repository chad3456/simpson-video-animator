POV: you finally get to clap back… and you don't 🕊️

After the war, Hanuman asked Sita for permission to punish the guards who had threatened her in Lanka. Sita said no: they were only following Ravana's orders, and no one never does wrong.

“पापानां वा शुभानां वा वधार्हाणां प्लवङ्गम । कार्यं करुणमार्येण न कश्चिन्नापराध्यति ॥”
Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda 116.44 (with 116.30–42)

Today's lesson comes from Sita. Mercy > revenge. Day 16 of learning from Rama.
