Rama's productivity checklist for Bharata, 2 kandas before 'that girl' routines existed 📋

1. Don't let sleep run you (Ayodhya Kanda 100.18)
2. Don't plan alone or with everyone; don't leak the plan (100.19)
3. Low effort, high reward? Start now (100.20)

“कच्चिदर्थं विनिश्चित्य लघुमूलं महोदयम् । क्षिप्रमारभसे कर्तुं न दीर्घयसि राघव ॥”

Verse: Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda 100.20 (check it at valmiki.iitk.ac.in). English rendering ours: Having settled on a goal that costs little and yields much, do you start on it quickly and not drag it out, Raghava?
