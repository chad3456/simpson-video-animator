Disagree with pyaar 🫶

In the Dandaka forest, Sita questioned Rama's vow to protect the sages: why fight without enmity? Rama thanked her, said she spoke out of love for his good, and explained that the sages had come to him for refuge and he had given his word.

“मुनीनामन्यथा कर्तुं सत्यमिष्टं हि मे सदा । अप्यहं जीवितं जह्यां त्वां वा सीते सलक्ष्मणाम् ॥”
Valmiki Ramayana, Aranya Kanda 10.18 (with Aranya 9 and 10.2–21)

Sabki suno, pyaar se. Par vaada mat todo. Day 15 of learning from Rama.
