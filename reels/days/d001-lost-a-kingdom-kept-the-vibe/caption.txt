Lost a kingdom overnight. Kept the vibe. 🏹

The night before his coronation, Rama is sent to the forest for 14 years. Valmiki says no one could see any change in his face.

“न वनं गन्तुकामस्य त्यजतश्च वसुन्धराम् । सर्वलोकातिगस्येव लक्ष्यते चित्तविक्रिया ॥”
Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda 19.33
