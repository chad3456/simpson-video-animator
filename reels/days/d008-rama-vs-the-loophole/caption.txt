With his father gone, the priest Jabali told Rama the promise was over: take the throne, go home, enjoy. Rama said it sounded right but was wrong, and stayed in the forest.

“सत्यमेवेश्वरो लोके सत्यं पद्मा श्रिता सदा । सत्यमूलानि सर्वाणि सत्यान्नास्ति परं पदम् ॥”
Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda 109.13

Truth is the root. Day 8 of learning from Rama.

#Ramayana #Valmiki #ShriRam #JaiShriRam #Truth #SatyamevaJayate #LifeLessons #Reels #SanskritShloka

Verse: Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda 109.13 (check it at valmiki.iitk.ac.in). English rendering ours: Truth alone is the lord in this world; prosperity always rests on truth. Truth is the root of everything; there is nothing higher than truth.
