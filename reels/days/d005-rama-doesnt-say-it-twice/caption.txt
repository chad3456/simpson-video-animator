Rama would never say 'bet' and then ghost 🫡

Before he even heard what his father wanted, Rama promised to do it. What his father wanted was 14 years in the forest. He kept it anyway.

“तद्ब्रूहि वचनं देवि राज्ञो यदभिकाङ्क्षितम् । करिष्ये प्रतिजाने च रामो द्विर्नाभिभाषते ॥”
Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda 18.30

Say it once. Mean it forever. Day 5 of learning from Rama.
