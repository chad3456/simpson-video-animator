Samundar mein do lakdiyaan 🪵🌊

When Bharata came to the forest grieving their father, Rama comforted him: every rise ends in a fall, every meeting in a parting. Family, friends and wealth come together and drift apart like two logs on the ocean.

“यथा काष्ठं च काष्ठं च समेयातां महार्णवे । समेत्य च व्यपेयातां कालमासाद्य कञ्चन ॥”
Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda 105.26 (with 105.16, 105.24–27)

Jo saath hain, unhe abhi pyaar karo. Day 10 of learning from Rama.
