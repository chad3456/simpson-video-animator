Rama's move after winning the war? Not a victory lap. 🤍

When Vibhishana grieved for his brother Ravana, Rama told him to give Ravana full last rites.

“मरणान्तानि वैराणि निर्वृत्तं नः प्रयोजनम् । क्रियतामस्य संस्कारो ममाप्येष यथा तव ॥”
Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda 112.26

When it's over, let it be over. Day 6 of learning from Rama.

#Ramayana #Valmiki #ShriRam #JaiShriRam #LetItGo #LifeLessons #Sanatan #Reels #SanskritShloka

Verse: Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda 112.26 (check it at valmiki.iitk.ac.in). English rendering ours: Enmity ends with death. Our purpose is done. Let his last rites be performed; he is as much mine as yours.
