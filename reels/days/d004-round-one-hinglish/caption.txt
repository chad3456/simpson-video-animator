Verse: Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda 59.143 (check it at valmiki.iitk.ac.in). English rendering ours: Go, I let you go, worn out by battle as you are. Enter Lanka, king of the night-rangers, catch your breath, then come out again with chariot and bow; then, from your chariot, you will see my strength.
