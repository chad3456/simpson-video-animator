The Ramayana opens with a GOAT checklist 🐐

Valmiki's first question to Narada: who in this world is virtuous and brave, grateful, truthful, kind to every being, free of anger and envy? Narada's answer: Rama, deep as the ocean and steady as the Himalaya (1.17).

“कोन्वस्मिन्साम्प्रतं लोके गुणवान्कश्च वीर्यवान् । धर्मज्ञश्च कृतज्ञश्च सत्यवाक्यो दृढव्रतः ॥”
Valmiki Ramayana, Bala Kanda 1.2 (with 1.3–4, 1.8, 1.17)
