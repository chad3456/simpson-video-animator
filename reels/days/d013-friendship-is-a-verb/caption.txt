Friendship is a verb 🤝

Sugriva, the exiled monkey king, told Rama his brother had thrown him out and taken his wife. Rama, who had just lost Sita himself, smiled and promised to help that same day.

“उपकारफलं मित्रमपकारोऽरिलक्षणम् । अद्यैव तं वधिष्यामि तव भार्यापहारिणम् ॥”
Valmiki Ramayana, Kishkindha Kanda 8.21

Help is the fruit of friendship. Don't just say 'let me know'. Show up. Day 13 of learning from Rama.
