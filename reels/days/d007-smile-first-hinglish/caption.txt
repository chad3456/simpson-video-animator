When King Dasharatha asked his court whether Rama should be crowned heir, the whole assembly said yes and listed what made him special.

“स्मितपूर्वाभिभाषी च धर्मं सर्वात्मना श्रितः । सम्यग्योक्ता श्रेयसां च न विगृह्य कथारुचिः ॥”
Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda 2.42

Smile first. Skip the drama. Day 7 of learning from Rama.
