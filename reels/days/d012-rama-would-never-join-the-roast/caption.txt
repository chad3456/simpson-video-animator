Kaikeyi was the reason Rama lost the throne and went into exile. When her son Bharata came to the forest blaming her, Rama hugged him, said he saw no fault in him, and told him not to blame his mother. Then he asked Bharata to respect her as much as their father.

“न दोषं त्वयि पश्यामि सूक्ष्ममप्यरिसूदन । न चापि जननीं बाल्यात्त्वं विगर्हितुमर्हसि ॥”
Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda 104.17 (with 104.15–21)
