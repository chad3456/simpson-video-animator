Rama's green flags, straight from Valmiki ✅

“कथञ्चिदुपकारेण कृतेनैकेन तुष्यति । न स्मरत्यपकाराणां शतमप्यात्मवत्तया ॥”
He is pleased by a single kindness and does not remember even a hundred wrongs. (Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda 1.11)

Keep the kindness. Delete the grudges.
