Even the villain's side rated him 10/10 🦌

When Ravana asked Maricha to help him carry off Sita, Maricha refused. He remembered young Rama sending him flying a hundred yojanas into the ocean with one arrow, and sparing his life. His verdict on Rama:

“रामो विग्रहवान् धर्मः साधुः सत्यपराक्रमः । राजा सर्वस्य लोकस्य देवानामिव वासवः ॥”
Valmiki Ramayana, Aranya Kanda 37.13 (the flashback is in 38.15–20)
