# The Dark Forest: bible

**Source.** Liu Cixin, *Remembrance of Earth's Past*: *The Three-Body Problem* (2006, trans. Ken Liu 2014), *The Dark Forest* (2008, trans. Joel Martinsen 2015), *Death's End* (2010, trans. Ken Liu 2016). The uploaded English editions of books 2 and 3 were used to check every quotation. Book 1 was not uploaded, so its events (Ye Zhetai's death, Silent Spring, Red Coast, the warning and Ye Wenjie's reply) are paraphrased, never quoted.

**Format.** LUMEN collage explainer in a Vox-style editorial look: real archival images as torn prints with name strips, handwritten notes, diagram kit (maps, tables, bars), hard cuts and punch-ins, brisk male narration (`am_michael:0.6+bm_george:0.4`, speed 1.14), no presenter. Fictional characters are drawn procedurally (not likenesses of any actor). No film or TV stills.

## Thesis

The Three-Body trilogy uses aliens to ask an old question: what happens to morality when survival is at stake? Its famous answer (the dark forest) is frightening, but the books end by undercutting it.

## Hook

Arecibo, 16 November 1974: we shouted our address into space. Why is the sky so quiet? Liu's answer: everyone is hiding.

## Critics (part 6)

- Noah Smith, "The dark forest hypothesis is absurd", *Noahpinion* (22 Jan 2024): space is transparent, so hiding fails; if hunters existed we would be gone; the logic makes genocide rational.
- David Brin, "The Great Silence", *QJRAS* 24 (1983): deadly hunters are one of many explanations for the quiet sky.
- Robert Axelrod, *The Evolution of Cooperation* (1984): in repeated games, nice-then-mirror strategies (tit for tat) beat ruthless ones.
- Chenchen Zhang, *Made in China Journal* (Dec 2023): the trilogy stages a false choice between morality and survival; reactionary readers made Cheng Xin a symbol of "feminised" compassion, which is misogynistic.

Shared ground: the books themselves end on cooperation (the Returners), so the critics and the text meet at the ending.

## Fairness notes

- The Cultural Revolution appears through real public-domain 1967 photographs of mass meetings, captioned as real; the characters are fictional and drawn.
- Hawking's 2010 remark and Liu's postscript are paraphrased, not quoted.
- The Arecibo, Green Bank, Pioneer, Voyager, Hubble, SDO, Apollo 8 and Pale Blue Dot images are NASA or openly licensed; the Green Bank dish is captioned as illustrative, not as the book's base.
