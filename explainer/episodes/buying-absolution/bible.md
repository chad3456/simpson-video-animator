# LUMEN E6 "Buying Absolution": episode bible

**Source:** *Suicidal Empathy: Dying to Be Kind*, Gad Saad (2026), chapter 6 ("Selling Indulgences"), read in full. No book text is stored in this repo; narration is paraphrase, with only short attributed phrases on screen.

**Rating:** general.

## Thesis and hook

- **Thesis (plain words):** Saad argues that DEI works like medieval indulgences: institutions pay to feel absolved, and competence and customers pay the price.
- **Hook:** Indulgences, the coffer slogan, Luther in 1517.

## The case against

1. Hiring discrimination is measurable (audit studies).
2. Wider outreach does not lower the bar.
3. Some programs were badly designed, and supporters say so.
4. Shared ground: open the doors wide, then pick the best.

## Hot-button handling

Named real companies and people only as reported facts; no logos or likenesses. Claims that are Saad's are introduced as his ("Saad argues", "according to Saad"); facts from public records are named with their source on screen or in the credits.
