# LUMEN E3 "Same Species, Different Rules": episode bible

**Source:** *Suicidal Empathy: Dying to Be Kind*, Gad Saad (2026), chapter 3 ("Cultural Theory of Mind"), read in full. No book text is stored in this repo; narration is paraphrase, with only short attributed phrases on screen.

**Rating:** mature (child sexual exploitation, FGM and honour killings discussed factually, no graphic detail).

## Thesis and hook

- **Thesis (plain words):** Saad argues that we wrongly assume every culture shares our values (a "categorization error"), and that this projection makes societies slow to name harmful practices.
- **Hook:** A hummingbird and a cassowary: both birds, very different rules.

## The case against

1. Cultures are not monoliths; reformers and dissidents exist everywhere.
2. Judge practices, not passports; collective suspicion has a dark history.
3. Second-generation integration data look like everyone else.
4. Shared ground: name the harm and protect victims without demonizing millions.

## Hot-button handling

Grooming gangs: told factually from the Jay Report and Casey Audit, with explicit note that the crimes were committed by particular men and condemned by British Muslim leaders. No creature stands in for any group. Claims that are Saad's are introduced as his ("Saad argues", "according to Saad"); facts from public records are named with their source on screen or in the credits.
