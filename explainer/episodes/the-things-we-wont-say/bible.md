# LUMEN E2 "The Things We Won’t Say": episode bible

**Source:** *Suicidal Empathy: Dying to Be Kind*, Gad Saad (2026), chapter 2 ("Forbidden Knowledge"), read in full. No book text is stored in this repo; narration is paraphrase, with only short attributed phrases on screen.

**Rating:** general.

## Thesis and hook

- **Thesis (plain words):** Saad argues that empathy is now the main excuse for hiding true findings and blurring plain words, and that a fact does not become false because it hurts.
- **Hook:** Galileo forced to recant, "protected" from a fact; then: we still do this, only now the reason is kindness.

| Idea (paraphrased) | Visual |

| Evil is real | The scorpion and the frog (original critters); the 2018 Tajikistan attack, named on screen only |

## The case against

1. Some caution about knowledge is legitimate (weapon recipes, research ethics).
2. New words are sometimes courtesy, not censorship.
3. His cases are contested: Harvard says Fryer was sanctioned over conduct; Housing First has trial evidence.
4. Shared ground: uncomfortable is not false; tone can be gentle without changing the facts.

## Hot-button handling

Fryer (both accounts given), the Tajikistan victims (named respectfully, Saad's "not mockery" framing kept). Claims that are Saad's are introduced as his ("Saad argues", "according to Saad"); facts from public records are named with their source on screen or in the credits.
