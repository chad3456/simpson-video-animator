# LUMEN E2 "Eight Nights of Fire": episode bible

**Event:** the riots that followed the police shooting of Nahel Merzouk, 17, in Nanterre on 27 June 2023 (main violence 27 June to 5 July). Chosen over the October 2026 cost-of-living unrest because the brief (radicals on the ground, the online Islamophobia storm, Saad's lens) describes 2023, and 2023 has settled official figures.

**Format:** single in-depth explainer, about 12 minutes, rated adult for its subject (violence, arson, a death), with stylised imagery only: no gore, no real people drawn, no logos. Same narrator, score and creatures as E1.

## Thesis and hook

- **Thesis:** the 2023 riots were cruel and costly, spread by phones, and then distorted online; Saad's "suicidal empathy" explains part of how France argued about them, but the data on French women only half fits it.
- **Hook:** a stopped yellow car, one shot, a passer-by's video, then eight nights.

## The lens (Part 6), attributed

From *Suicidal Empathy* (paraphrased): empathy misfires when too high or aimed at the wrong target; in France, "les jeunes" is a euphemism that, he claims, hides a link to Islamic immigration; women score higher on empathy and are, he argues, more prone to suicidal empathy. The application to 2023 is labelled on screen as our reading of his lens, not his words.

**The women claim, tested:** the Ifop gender split shows women a bit more likely to "understand" the violence, equally unlikely to approve of it, and slightly warmer to the police. The episode says it fits half.

## Fairness rules applied

- Rioters are hooded grey pebbles: anonymous, never marked by religion, skin or nationality. Police wear caps. Residents and victims are the usual bright pebbles.
- Every count is on screen with a source chip. Where sources differ (90% vs 71% French), both are shown.
- Religion is discussed only as a claim (Saad's, and online posts), with what the record does and doesn't show.
- The online Islamophobia is shown as it was (volumes, a quoted claim, fakes) and attributed to fact-checkers.
- Critics get a full part. The shared ground closes the episode.
