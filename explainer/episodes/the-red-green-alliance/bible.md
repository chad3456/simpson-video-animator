# LUMEN Investigation 1 "The Red-Green Alliance": episode bible

**Brief:** the requester asked for "a detailed, serious investigation video based on facts" on the Red-Green Alliance as covered by Asra Nomani. The episode presents her case fairly and in her framing, then tests each part against court records, government actions, data and named critics. All facts as of 23 October 2026.

**Format:** a single explainer of about 11 minutes, rated general. Same LUMEN narrator, score and pebbles. No real people are drawn, no group is personified, and Islam (the faith) is kept visually and verbally distinct from Islamism (the political movement), a distinction Nomani herself insists on.

**Device:** every claim on screen gets an evidence stamp:

| Stamp | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ON THE RECORD (mint) | confirmed by a court, a government or published data |
| HER REPORTING (sky) | documents Nomani cites that we could not check independently |
| NOMANI'S ANALYSIS (amber) | her interpretation of the facts |
| DISPUTED (pink) | other credible evidence points the other way |
| NOT SUPPORTED (red) | no evidence found, or contradicted |

## Thesis and hook

- **Thesis:** cooperation between parts of the hard left and Islamist-linked organisations is real and partly documented, and so are some of the money trails. The claim that they form one coordinated army that captured a party, steers protests or drove France's riots is Nomani's analysis, built mostly from networks of shared causes and social media activity, and it is disputed. Muslims as a group are not part of it, and the data show they are not a bloc.
- **Hook:** a red circle and a green circle sliding together. An alliance, or a label?

## Critics and the other side

- **CAIR:** a civil rights group, never charged, calls the Texas label defamatory and is suing.
- **French academics and the CNRS:** the label has no scientific definition and works as a smear.
- **Method:** link maps built from likes, events and shared causes show agreement, not command.
- **Data:** Muslim Americans split politically and are socially conservative on many issues, so a merger with the progressive left is narrow and fragile. Nomani concedes the turnout gap.
- **Her side, fairly:** the cooperation is real, CAIR's court-record ties are real, and the foreign money is real and large.
